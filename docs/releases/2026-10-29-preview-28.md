# Orchard 0.1.0-preview.20261029.28

This preview accepts Field Help Tooltip on x64. The package-matched extracted
catalog passed its normal, hover, pressed, disabled and empty states in Light
and Dark, physical hover/click/Enter/Space/focus, and reduced-motion behavior.
The independent NuGet consumer compiled and ran; four native 150% Light/Dark
narrow/wide cases kept the trigger and bottom tooltip within their bounds.

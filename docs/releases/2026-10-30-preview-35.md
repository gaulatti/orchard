# Orchard 0.1.0-preview.20261030.35

This preview adds Filter Chip and Filter Group to the x64 accepted list. They
pass the five component checks: applicable Light/Dark states, keyboard,
pointer and focus behavior, native 150% no-clipping, reduced motion, and
independent package-consumer compilation. The fixed 112-entry inventory has
**42 accepted** and 70 not yet accepted. Monitoring and Editorial remain
fictional local-data component demos.

  changing OS settings.

  typography. Its native island passed six 150% standalone, grouped and RTL

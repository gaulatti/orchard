# Orchard 0.1.0-preview.20261029.31

This preview adds Switch to the x64 accepted list. Its applicable Light/Dark
states, physical keyboard/pointer/focus paths, 150% layout, reduced-motion
behavior, and independent package consumer all pass. The fixed 112-entry
inventory has **36 accepted** and 76 not yet accepted. Monitoring and Editorial
remain fictional local-data component demos.

states, physical keyboard/pointer/focus behavior, 150% no-clipping, reduced
motion, and independent package-consumer compilation pass. The fixed
112-entry inventory has **50 accepted** and 62 not yet accepted. Monitoring
and Editorial remain fictional local-data component demos.

## Validation and artifacts

- The package-matching extracted x64 catalog passed 134 Theme Toggle states in
  Light and Dark. Physical pointer, Space, Enter and focus behavior passed in
  both themes, including consumer-owned mode requests, disabled rejection and
  remount. With animations disabled, six pressed states reached the immediate
  endpoint and twelve hover/focus states remained at rest.
- The independent x64 NuGet consumer compiled and ran. Its visible native
  150% island passed 108 Light/Dark cases across three modes, sizes and variants
  with enabled/disabled states; icons stayed inside the owner bounds.
- The extracted x64 catalog passed 72 hidden Monitoring and Editorial demo checks.
- The repository's 498 portable and 10 Windows text tests passed.

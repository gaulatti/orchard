states, physical keyboard/pointer/focus behavior, 150% no-clipping, reduced
motion, and independent package-consumer compilation pass. The fixed
112-entry inventory has **52 accepted** and 60 not yet accepted. Monitoring
and Editorial remain fictional local-data component demos.

## Validation and artifacts

- The package-matching independent x64 consumer passed 60 OTP source states,
  32 controlled behavior cases, and physical pointer/keyboard/focus checks in
  Light and Dark. The extracted x64 catalog passed both OTP theme cases.

  passed ten Light/Dark cases across normal, secure, disabled, large-font and
  RTL layouts; all six slots and their editors stayed in bounds.

  focus rings snap to their endpoint in both themes. No OS setting was changed.
- The x64 catalog passed 72 hidden fictional Monitoring and Editorial demo checks.

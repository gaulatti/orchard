## Validation and artifacts

- Stepper passed 22 Light and 22 Dark source-state cases in the .49 x64
  catalog, including horizontal, vertical, RTL, narrow and status variants.
  Its baseline now uses Stepper-local coordinates, removing a fractional
  catalog-host offset from text alignment.
- The same x64 catalog passed native pointer ownership, Tab and held-Space
  input, focus, disabled-step rejection, original-step callbacks, reorder and
  lifecycle checks in both themes. The independent .49 package consumer
  passed eight native 150% Light/Dark layouts with no clipped steps or labels.
  With animations already disabled, changed status colors settled immediately
  and stayed static; no OS setting was changed.
- The fresh .49 independent x64 NuGet consumer compiled and ran. The x64
  catalog ZIP was extracted and byte-verified across 664 files, contains the
  package's exact Orchard.dll, and passed 72 isolated fictional Monitoring and
  Editorial demo checks with no resource or binding failures.
- The ARM64 catalog built; its ZIP was extracted and byte-verified across 660
  files with the same package DLL. The independent ARM64 consumer compiled.
  ARM64 runtime is unverified.
- The repository's 498 portable and 10 Windows text tests passed.

Dark states, decorative keyboard/pointer/focus behavior, 150% no-clipping,
reduced motion, and independent package-consumer compilation pass. The fixed

## Validation and artifacts

- The package-matching extracted x64 catalog passed all four Bauhaus variants,
  live image and background ownership, decorative input/focus semantics, and
  reduced motion in Light and Dark. Captured windows show the rendered states;
  exact pixel-color equality is outside this milestone.
- The independent x64 NuGet consumer compiled and ran. Its visible native
  150% island passed eight Light/Dark and wide/square/tall/narrow cases with
  clipping and composition output inside each consumer-owned surface.
- The extracted x64 catalog passed 72 hidden Monitoring and Editorial demo checks.
- The repository's 498 portable and 10 Windows text tests passed.

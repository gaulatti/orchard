## Validation and artifacts

- Timeline passed 72 Light/Dark source-state cases at 639, 640 and 1024 DIPs
  in the exact .50 x64 catalog, with zero failures. Native pointer, Space,
  Enter, focus, original icon ownership, keyed lifecycle and live breakpoint
  checks passed in both themes.
- The independent .50 x64 NuGet consumer compiled and ran. Eight native 150%
  compact/wide and RTL layouts kept rows, icons and text in bounds. With
  animations already disabled, status colors settled immediately and stayed
  static; no OS setting was changed.
- The .50 x64 catalog ZIP was extracted and byte-verified across 664 files.
  Its Orchard.dll matches the package, and the extracted fictional Monitoring
  and Editorial demos passed 72 isolated checks.
- The ARM64 independent consumer compiled, and the ARM64 catalog ZIP was
  extracted and byte-verified across 660 files with the same package DLL.
  ARM64 runtime is unverified.
- The unchanged library source passed 498 portable and 10 Windows text tests
  during the preceding .49 preview; .50 changes acceptance fixtures and docs.

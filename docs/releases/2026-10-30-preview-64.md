## Validation and artifacts

- The exact .64 independent x64 package consumer compiled and ran. Its
  Popover audit passed 122 Light/Dark layout states and five lifecycle
  contracts; the hosted modal fixture passed six more public-API cases.
- The extracted .64 x64 catalog passed real pointer, keyboard, focus, Escape,
  outside dismissal, nested and modal interactions in Light and Dark.
- The independent .64 package consumer passed four native 150% popup-surface
  and caller-text layouts at 320/480 DIP across Light/Dark with no clipping.
  The component has no animation; its layout settled while Windows animations
  were already disabled. The first fixture measured before late layout settled;
  the corrected fixture passed without changing the component.
- The x64 catalog ZIP was stream-verified across 664 files; its extracted
  Orchard.dll matches the NuGet package. The fictional Monitoring and Editorial
  demos passed 72 isolated checks.
- The ARM64 catalog built and its ZIP was stream-verified across 660 files.
  An independent ARM64 package consumer compiled. ARM64 runtime is unverified.
- The library implementation is unchanged. The previously passed 498 portable
  and 10 Windows text tests are reused; .64 changes acceptance fixtures,
  a package-audit receipt handoff, and documentation only.

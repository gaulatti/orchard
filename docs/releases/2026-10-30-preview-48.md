# Orchard 0.1.0-preview.20261030.48

This preview adds Scroll Area to the x64 accepted list. Its Light and Dark
states, physical keyboard/pointer/focus behavior, 150% no-clipping, reduced
motion, and independent package-consumer compilation pass. The fixed
112-entry inventory has **55 accepted** and 57 not yet accepted. Monitoring
and Editorial remain fictional local-data component demos.

## Validation and artifacts

- The independent x64 NuGet consumer compiled and ran against the fresh .48
  package. Scroll Area passed 94 Light/Dark native layouts, 12 controlled
  scrolling histories and five contract groups with no failures.
- Ten native 150% Light/Dark consumer layouts passed across vertical,
  horizontal, both, RTL and empty cases. Owner and viewport stayed in bounds;
  caller content remained intact. With Windows animations already disabled,
  immediate offsets remained static in those cases; no OS setting was changed.
- The unchanged Scroll Area code and catalog fixture passed physical pointer,
  wheel, Tab and Down-key scrolling in the extracted .47 x64 catalog. The
  accessibility snapshot's focused-element field was stale, so the focus
  claim rests on visible outline and observable key response.
- The .48 x64 catalog ZIP was freshly extracted and byte-verified across 664
  files. Its Orchard DLL matches the package, and the extracted fictional
  Monitoring and Editorial demos passed 72 checks with no resource or binding
  errors.
- The ARM64 catalog built; its ZIP was extracted and byte-verified across 660
  files with the same package DLL. The ARM64 independent consumer compiled.
  ARM64 runtime is unverified.
- The repository's 498 portable and 10 Windows text tests passed.

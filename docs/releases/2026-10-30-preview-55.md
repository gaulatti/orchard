## Validation and artifacts

- The extracted .55 x64 catalog passed 66 Light/Dark Pagination frames and
  four physical keyboard/pointer/focus sequences. Its 64 controlled
  reduced-motion cases passed with animations disabled.
- Tabs passed both themes across nine variant/size pairs, controlled selection,
  disabled and navigation behavior, and physical keyboard, pointer and focus.
- The independent .55 x64 NuGet consumer compiled and ran. Twenty-four native
  150% Pagination layouts cover both themes, two widths, three page positions
  and RTL/LTR without clipped actions. Thirty-six native 150% Tabs layouts
  cover both themes, three variants, three sizes and two widths without clipped
  buttons. Tabs selection paint and positions stayed static with reduced motion.
- The x64 catalog ZIP was stream-verified across 664 files, extracted, and its
  Orchard.dll matched the package. The extracted fictional Monitoring and
  Editorial demos passed 72 isolated checks.
- The ARM64 catalog built and its ZIP was stream-verified across 660 files. The
  independent ARM64 package consumer compiled. ARM64 runtime is unverified.
- The unchanged library source passed 498 portable and 10 Windows text tests
  during .49; .55 changes only package-consumer acceptance fixtures and docs.

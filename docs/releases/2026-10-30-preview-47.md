## Validation and artifacts

- The package-matching independent x64 consumer passed 58 App Tab Bar
  destination, badge, icon-source and lifecycle cases plus eight Light/Dark
  rendered layouts. The existing physical x64 catalog checkpoint passed
  pointer, keyboard and focus paths; the component and fixture are unchanged.
- The independent x64 NuGet consumer compiled and ran. Its native 150% island
  passed eight Light/Dark layouts across normal, narrow, enlarged-label and
  RTL states; destinations, icons, labels and badges stayed in bounds.
- App Tab Bar has no animation or transition path. With Windows animations
  already disabled, destination bounds stayed static in all eight layouts;
  no OS setting was changed.

- The repository's 498 portable and 10 Windows text tests passed.

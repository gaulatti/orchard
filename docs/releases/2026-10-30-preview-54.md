- Search Input passed 40 base and 48 interaction/paint states across Light/Dark
  and 320/640-DIP clients from the extracted .54 x64 catalog. Physical pointer,
  keyboard, focus and clear-action paths passed with animations disabled.
- The independent .54 x64 NuGet consumer compiled and ran. Twenty-four native
  150% Light/Dark layouts cover three sizes, error/no-error states and both
  widths; the editor and clear action stayed in bounds and stable with reduced
  motion. No OS setting was changed.

  Orchard.dll matched the package. The extracted fictional Monitoring and
  Editorial demos passed 72 isolated checks.

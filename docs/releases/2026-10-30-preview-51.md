# Orchard 0.1.0-preview.20261030.51

This preview adds Loading Overlay to the x64 accepted list. Its Light and
Dark states, physical keyboard/pointer/focus behavior, native 150% no-clipping,
reduced motion, and independent package-consumer compilation pass. The fixed
112-entry inventory has **58 accepted** and 54 not yet accepted. Monitoring
and Editorial remain fictional local-data component demos.

## Validation and artifacts

- Loading Overlay passed 60 Light/Dark source cases at 320×240, 640×480 and
  1024×900 DIPs in the exact .51 x64 catalog, with zero failures. Physical
  pointer, Space/Enter, Escape, focus transfer and Tab restoration passed in
  both themes. Its fixture now checks the observable result after WinUI clears
  a temporary root focus following a portal click.
- The fresh independent .51 x64 NuGet consumer compiled and ran. Four native
  150% Light/Dark layouts kept the portal, card, spinner and label in bounds.
  With animations already disabled, the spinner stayed static; no OS setting
  was changed.
- The .51 x64 catalog ZIP was extracted and byte-verified across 664 files.
  Its Orchard.dll matches the package, and the extracted fictional Monitoring
  and Editorial demos passed 72 isolated checks.
- The ARM64 independent consumer compiled, and the ARM64 catalog ZIP was
  extracted and byte-verified across 660 files with the same package DLL.
  ARM64 runtime is unverified.
- The unchanged library source passed 498 portable and 10 Windows text tests
  during the .49 preview; .51 changes the catalog acceptance fixture and docs.

physical pointer/keyboard/focus behavior, 150% no-clipping, reduced motion,
and independent package-consumer compilation pass. The fixed 112-entry
inventory has **48 accepted** and 64 not yet accepted. Monitoring and Editorial

## Validation and artifacts

- The package-matching extracted x64 catalog passed all four Alert types,
  empty and long messages, constrained width, live theme changes, and reduced
  motion in Light and Dark. Physical pointer close, cancelled held press,
  Tab, Shift+Tab, Space, focus preservation, and caller callbacks passed.
- The independent x64 NuGet consumer compiled and ran. Its visible native
  150% island showed a wrapped long message and close action fully within the
  448px Alert surface in Light and Dark.

- The repository's 498 portable and 10 Windows text tests passed.

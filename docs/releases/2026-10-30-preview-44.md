# Orchard 0.1.0-preview.20261030.44

This preview adds Segmented Control to the x64 accepted list. Its Light and Dark
states, physical keyboard/pointer/focus behavior, 150% no-clipping, reduced
motion, and independent package-consumer compilation pass. The fixed
112-entry inventory has **51 accepted** and 61 not yet accepted. Monitoring
and Editorial remain fictional local-data component demos.

## Validation and artifacts

- The package-matching independent x64 consumer passed 40 Segmented Control
  bound-selection cases and eight Light/Dark layouts. Physical pointer,
  keyboard, focus and RTL selection passed in both themes. The extracted x64
  catalog passed its Light and Dark Segmented Control cases.
- The independent x64 NuGet consumer compiled and ran. Its native 150% island
  passed 16 Light/Dark cases across natural, narrow, large-text and RTL layouts
  with two selection states; frames, buttons and labels stayed in bounds.
- Selection uses immediate VisualState setters with transitions disabled and no
  storyboard, so reduced motion has no alternate animation to suppress.
- The x64 catalog passed 72 hidden fictional Monitoring and Editorial demo checks.
- The repository's 498 portable and 10 Windows text tests passed.
- The ARM64 catalog and independent consumer compiled. ARM64 runtime is unverified.

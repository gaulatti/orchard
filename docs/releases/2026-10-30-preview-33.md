# Orchard 0.1.0-preview.20261030.33

This preview adds Radio Group and Slider to the x64 accepted list. Both pass
the five component checks: applicable Light/Dark states, physical
keyboard/pointer/focus, native 150% no-clipping, reduced motion, and independent
package-consumer compilation. The fixed 112-entry inventory has **39 accepted**
and 73 not yet accepted. Monitoring and Editorial remain fictional local-data
component demos.

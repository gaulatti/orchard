Data Table Column Toggle passes the five x64 checks on the exact .78 package and matching catalog: Light/Dark source and lifecycle states; physical trigger, checkbox, Space, Tab, Shift+Tab, Escape, outside click and focus behavior; four native 150% 400/700 DIP cases with the open popup and checkbox options in bounds; static popup geometry and opacity on an animations-disabled desktop; and independent package-consumer compilation. The ARM64 catalog and consumer compile, with runtime unverified.
